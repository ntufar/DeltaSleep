Sleep
unplugged.

A free, open-source sleep and snore tracker. It has no INTERNET permission, so it cannot send a single byte.

DeltaSleep scores your night on the phone, then deletes the audio. Nights and trends stay in one private file.

What the phone works out overnight

Sleep stages

Awake, light, deep and estimated REM, every 30 seconds. The phone uses movement and breathing sounds. You do not need a wearable.

Snore detection

The app measures band power from 20 to 300 Hz. It marks each snore event with an intensity from 1 to 5. It saves no audio clip.

Apnea screening

A breathing-pause report you can show to a doctor. It is a screening aid, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose.

Trends

See sleep time, deep sleep and snore share over 7, 30 and 90 nights. Export every epoch as CSV to a folder you choose.

Quiet on battery and music

The target is 3% battery for an 8 hour night. Spotify, Audible and podcasts keep playing, because the app mixes with other audio.

  1. You tap Sleep.

    The microphone calibrates to the room noise. There is no account and no prompt to sign in.

  2. A Rust core listens.

    It reads 10 ms frames and measures level, zero crossings and low-band power.

  3. The audio is gone.

    Every 30 seconds the app writes one label to local SQLite. It discards the raw sound at once.

  4. You wake to a chart.

    Tap Stop. The hypnogram appears with no sync and no spinner, because there is no server.

Type any server. Ask DeltaSleep to send your night there.
