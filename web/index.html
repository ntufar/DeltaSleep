Features

Everything your night has to tell you

The phone listens to movement and breathing sounds, then writes a short summary and discards the audio.

Sleep stages

Awake, light, deep and estimated REM in 30 second epochs. You do not need a wearable.

Snore detection

Band power from 20 to 300 Hz marks each snore with an intensity from 1 to 5. The app saves no audio clip.

Apnea screening

A breathing-pause report to show a doctor. It is a screening aid, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose.

Trends

Sleep time, deep sleep and snore share over 7, 30 and 90 nights.

Your data, your folder

Export every epoch as CSV to a folder you choose. Delete everything at any time.

Quiet on battery and music

Spotify, Audible and podcasts keep playing, because the app mixes with other audio.

The app

Clear charts, no spinner

Tap Stop and the result is there. There is no sync, because there is no server.

How it works

From microphone to chart, on the device

  1. You tap Sleep

    The microphone calibrates to the room noise. There is no account and no sign-in.

  2. A Rust core listens

    It reads 10 ms frames and measures level, zero crossings and low-band power.

  3. The audio is gone

    Every 30 seconds the app writes one label to local SQLite and discards the raw sound.

  4. You wake to a chart

    Tap Stop. The hypnogram appears at once.

Privacy you can check

Ask it to send your night somewhere

Type any server. Android refuses the connection, because the app does not declare the INTERNET permission.

CI also fails the build if http, socket or fetch appears in the source.
